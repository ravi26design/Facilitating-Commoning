## What the licences require

- **CC BY 2.0** (`forests.jpg`) — credit the photographer, link the licence,
  note that the image was cropped. Any use is fine, including commercial.
- **CC BY-SA 4.0** (`pastureland.jpg`, `water.jpg`) — same, **plus** the
  ShareAlike term: if you publish a modified version of the *image*, that
  modified image must carry the same licence. ShareAlike attaches to the
  photograph, not to the surrounding page or code.

## People in these photographs

`hero-commoning.jpg` and `story-gram-sabha-forest.jpg` show identifiable people
at village assemblies.
The photographs are freely licensed for reuse, but a licence is not the same
as the subjects' consent to appear in FES marketing — and the hero image is the
most prominent placement on the site. Before this goes live, replace both with
photographs FES has consent for.

## Recommended replacement

These are stand-ins chosen to show the design working with real photography.
For the live site, prefer **FES's own field photographs** — they will show the
actual commons and communities this repository serves, carry no third-party
licence obligation, and are more honest to the content. Drop replacements in
at the same filenames and delete this file.
